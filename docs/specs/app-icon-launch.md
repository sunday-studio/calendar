# App icon and launch screen (no board: designed here from the canvas language)

Brief: the canvas' signature is the **split-flap time** and the **amber now line**. The icon is a flip-cell time on near-black, with the split line in amber.

## App icon (1024×1024, no transparency, no rounding: iOS masks it)
**Dark variant** — background `#0A0A0B`. Five flip cells reading **10:30** (the sample time used across the canvas), centred, cell **148 × 420**, gap **14**, total width 5×148 + 4×14 = **796**, left/right margin 114, vertical centre 512. Cell face `#26262A`; glyph `#F4F1EA` in the Mono Medium face, size **215 pt**, centred in the cell and drawn **over** the split line; the colon cell shows a colon glyph. **Split line**: 10 px tall, across the full width of every cell at the vertical midpoint, **amber `#F0B84A`** (this is the one deviation from the in-app `#050505` split: the amber line is "now" and reads at small sizes). No gradient, no gloss, no corner radius on cells, no border.
**Light variant** — background `#F1F1EE`; cells `#0A0A0B`; glyphs `#F4F1EA`; split line amber `#8A5A00`.
**Tinted variant** (iOS 18+ monochrome): background black, cells white 100% alpha at 85%, glyphs black, split line white at 40% (the system tints it).
Small-size check: at 60×60 the five cells must stay distinguishable (cell width ≥ 8.4px): they are.
Mapping in `AppIcon.appiconset/Contents.json` (Xcode 16 single-size universal): *Any appearance* = `AppIcon-light.png`, *Dark* = `AppIcon-dark.png`, *Tinted* = `AppIcon-tinted.png`. Generated by `scripts/make-icons.swift` (committed, deterministic).

## Launch screen
Static (Info.plist `UILaunchScreen`), no animation, and it must hand off to the first screen without a jump:
- Background colour set **LaunchBackground**: light `#F1F1EE`, dark `#0A0A0B` (= `Theme.bg`).
- Image **LaunchMark**: the wordmark **CALENDAR** in flip cells at the `xl` size (cell 26 × 60 incl. gap, glyph Mono Medium 44) — 8 cells, 211 × 60 pt — centred; light/dark variants (cells `#26262A` on both backgrounds, glyph `#F4F1EA`; the split line `#050505`). `UIImageRespectsSafeAreaInsets = false`.
- Info.plist: `UILaunchScreen: { UIColorName: LaunchBackground, UIImageName: LaunchMark, UIImageRespectsSafeAreaInsets: false }`.
- First frame after launch is the saved agenda (no spinner) per h-load; when a first launch has no data, the Home "first load" skeleton appears.
Files: `Assets.xcassets/LaunchBackground.colorset`, `Assets.xcassets/LaunchMark.imageset` (@2x, @3x PNG, light + dark).

## Wiring (needs a Swift-lane edit in `ios/project.yml`)
`settings.base.ASSETCATALOG_COMPILER_APPICON_NAME: AppIcon`, `ASSETCATALOG_COMPILER_INCLUDE_ALL_APPICON_ASSETS: YES` (for dark/tinted), and replace `UILaunchScreen: {}` with the dict above. Widgets/NSE need no icon.

## Done means
Home-screen screenshots of the app icon in light, dark and tinted appearance, and a cold-launch recording frame showing the launch image matching the first Home frame (same background, no flash).
